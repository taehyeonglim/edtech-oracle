# MODE_F_LOGO — Moderator emblem

## Subject
A minimal, flat vector emblem representing a neutral **moderator** who runs a structured debate between scholars. It is NOT a person: no face, no human figure, no portrait. The mark is purely geometric and symbolic.

## Composition
Centered symbol on a solid background. Two mirrored speech-bubble shapes face each other from left and right; between them sits a single upright vertical element reading as an abstract lectern/microphone stem — a slim vertical bar rising into a small rounded cap, with a narrow horizontal base. The bubbles are simple rounded rectangles with a single tail each, equal in size and weight, so neither side dominates. A thin horizontal baseline connects the composition. Generous empty margin on all four sides: the symbol occupies roughly the central 55% of the frame so that a circular crop never clips it.

## Style
Flat vector illustration. No gradients, no photorealism, no 3D, no drop shadows, no texture, no bevel. Uniform stroke weight throughout. Geometric, calm, institutional — the visual language of a wordmark in a reference work, not a consumer app icon.

## Color
Solid deep navy-charcoal background (#2E323C). Symbol in a soft warm off-white / pale grey (#DDE1E8). One single accent: the central lectern stem in a muted slate blue (#7C93B8). Strictly three colors total.

## Technical Specifications
Square 1:1 aspect ratio, minimum 1024x1024. High quality, crisp clean edges, perfectly symmetrical left-to-right. Vector-clean rendering suitable for display at 64px.

## Text Integration
No text of any kind. No letters, no numerals, no lettermark, no signature, no watermark.

## Anti-Patterns
Avoid: any human face or silhouette, hands, eyes, robots, gavels, scales of justice, courtroom imagery, chat-app iconography, glossy web-2.0 style, gradients, drop shadows, busy detail, off-center composition, symbol touching the frame edges, decorative flourishes, text.
