# MODE_F_LOGO — Brand mark for an evidence-cited knowledge wiki

## Subject
A minimal, flat vector brand emblem for a scholarly reference project called an "oracle": a knowledge base where every claim carries a cited source. The mark fuses **an open book** with **a constellation** — knowledge that is both recorded and navigated by fixed points.

## Composition
Centered emblem on a solid background. The lower half is a simplified open book seen straight on: two symmetrical trapezoid pages meeting at a central spine, drawn as clean geometric planes with a single fold line each. Rising directly above the open pages is a small constellation — five or six evenly weighted dots joined by thin straight lines into a simple asterism, with one dot slightly larger as the anchor star. The constellation sits centered above the spine, as though read out of the book. Generous empty margin: the emblem occupies roughly the central 55% of the frame so a circular crop never clips it.

## Style
Flat vector illustration, emblem/crest logic. No gradients, no photorealism, no 3D, no drop shadows, no paper texture, no glow. Uniform stroke weight. Geometric and restrained — an academic imprint mark, not an app icon.

## Color
Solid deep navy background (#141F35). Book rendered in warm ivory (#F0E6D2). Constellation dots and connecting lines in a bright muted gold (#D9A441). Strictly three colors total.

## Technical Specifications
Square 1:1 aspect ratio, minimum 1024x1024. High quality, crisp clean edges, symmetrical left-to-right. Vector-clean rendering that stays legible at 64px.

## Text Integration
No text of any kind. No letters, no numerals, no wordmark, no signature, no watermark.

## Anti-Patterns
Avoid: human faces, eyes, crystal balls, mystical or occult imagery, owls, graduation caps, lightbulbs, gears, glossy gradients, drop shadows, realistic book photography, busy star fields, off-center composition, emblem touching the frame edges, text.
